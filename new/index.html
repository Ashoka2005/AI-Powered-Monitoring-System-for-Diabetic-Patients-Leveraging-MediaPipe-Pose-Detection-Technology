    ℹ️ Major Measurement Parameters

    📏 Biometric Metrics

    • BMI (Body Mass Index): Calculated from height and weight. Categorizes metabolic risk levels (Underweight, Normal, Overweight).

    📐 Real-time Kinematics

    • Joint Angles: Real-time monitoring of Shoulder, Elbow, Hip, Knee, Ankle, and Spine angles using 33 3D body landmarks.
    • Form Accuracy: Percentage match between patient's real-time pose and the therapeutic target angle.

    🧪 Metabolic Indicators

    • Glycemic-Impact Score: A proprietary metric calculated as (Accuracy × 0.8) + (Reps × 2.0). Estimates the glucose-regulating effectiveness of the session.
    • Patient Level Monitoring:
      • Level 1 (Maintenance): Score > 70
      • Level 2 (Targeted): Score 40-70
      • Level 3 (Strict): Score < 40
    • Repetition Count: Tracks volume of movement for metabolic expenditure tracking.

    🚶 Walk & Body Movement

    • Step Symmetry: Compares left and right knee extension angles to detect uneven gait or limping.
    • Gait Stability: Monitors spinal sway (Trunk Stability) to ensure balanced walking patterns.
    • Shoulder Alignment: Detects lateral tilting during movement, a key indicator of gait imbalance.
